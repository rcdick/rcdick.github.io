To begin to populate this page, I will be writing a series of essays with the overarching title _Ground Truth_. The idea behind this somewhat high-handedly chosen title is to work through, in my mind, the disconnect between disciplines and fields I think should obviously talk to each other better than they do so now. I aim to explore science and policy, and the connections they try to make with the people they impact. I also attempt to translate between the two. There will not be any overarching thread, rather these essays will be standalone pieces that might overlap here and there. 

They will also be longer than these first two posts to the repo.
