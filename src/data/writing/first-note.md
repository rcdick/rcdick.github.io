I've been thinking for a while that I should have someplace to put my ideas before they disappear. 

I need the incentive, rather the pressure, of publishing, but will not particularly worry much whether anyone ends up reading this. 

The purpose of this site is twofold: a) to be a space in which I can deposit, file away, and occasionally continually build upon ideas, concepts, and plans I happen to have, and b) to drive me to write. To not exclusively think, but to - in the most literal sense of the word - commit to paper these thoughts and opinions. 

As a disclaimer, as the about page already details: some of this will be incomplete, wrong, or a combination of both.
